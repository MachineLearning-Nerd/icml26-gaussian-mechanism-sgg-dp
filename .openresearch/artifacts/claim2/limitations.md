# Limitations and deviations

The certificate is an independently reconstructed symbolic derivation, not a
Lean/Coq kernel proof. Its imported facts—orthogonal isometry, Haar left
invariance, and invariance of hockey-stick divergence under measurable
bijections—are standard defining properties and are enumerated rather than
hidden.

The old Gaussian instance remains useful only as numerical corroboration and is
explicitly labeled a historical rejected baseline.
