# Limitations and deviations

The universal evidence is a structured symbolic certificate, not a
Lean/Coq kernel proof. Standard probability facts (Gaussian representation of
a uniform sphere direction, Slutsky, Pólya's theorem, and mixture/data
processing properties of hockey-stick divergence) are explicit imported
lemmas.

The finite-`T` sweep does not establish the universal theorem and is never
labeled as doing so. The paper itself states that no explicit `T0` or
convergence rate is provided.
